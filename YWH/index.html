01ABOUT ME

Research rooted in security.
Driven by curiosity.

I am a doctoral student affiliated with the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences (UCAS) and the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences (IIE, CAS).

Previously, I studied at Hebei University. My published work covers dynamic cross-domain authentication for Internet of Things (IoT) systems and knowledge-graph-based approaches to cybersecurity talent selection.

This page lists publicly verifiable publications only. Ongoing and unpublished work is intentionally not described as accepted or published research.
