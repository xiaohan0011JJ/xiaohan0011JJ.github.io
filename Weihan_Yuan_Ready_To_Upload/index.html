About me

I am a doctoral student at the University of Chinese Academy of Sciences, affiliated with the Institute of Information Engineering, Chinese Academy of Sciences.

I previously studied at Hebei University. My work spans practical security questions in authentication systems, privacy, and knowledge-driven computation.
